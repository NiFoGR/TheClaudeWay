# The closing funnel (DRAFT for Nik to review)

Goal: turn a scraped lead into a paying client **by email and demo, without a call where possible**, as fast as
possible. Calls only happen when they help close (a warm lead, or someone unsure). Built from the System Plan, the
playbook (one goal per step, low threshold, easy to say yes, follow up until they buy, die or say no) and what's built.

## Why this should close fast

- **The demo is built before the first contact**, so the very first email already shows the result; nothing to wait for.
- **Buying doesn't need a call.** The order page answers the usual objections in writing (price, "think about it",
  "already have a website", "which clients"), and the price is shown plainly.
- **Every step has one goal and one button.** Email → demo. Demo → "Make it mine". Order page → pay.
- **Real urgency only**: the preview expiry date and the weekend deal. Both are true.
- **You only spend time where it pays**: hot signals and unsure replies, with the script already filled in.

## What we measure (per stage, shown on Outreach)

Delivered → demo viewed → order page opened → paid; and replies → calls → paid. The weakest stage is what we fix next
(copy, the demo's look, the order page, or the leads).

2. **Price on the demo bar or only on the order page?** (I'd say only on the order page: the demo sells the result first.)
3. **Request-a-call button on the order page, or only offered in replies?** (I'd show a small "Got a question? Get a
   call back" under the Order button: some trades won't buy without a voice.)
4. **How should hot signals reach your phone?** Free options: a Telegram bot message, the free ntfy app (push
   notification), or an email to your phone. (I'd say Telegram: instant, free, works on any phone.)
5. **Order started but not paid:** Claude emails them the next morning ("anything I can help with?")? (I'd say yes.)
6. **Founding-client deal** for the first 3–5 clients in exchange for a testimonial: what's it worth to you?
   (e.g. first month of the retainer free on top, or £150 off.)
7. **Demo look:** one template per trade (as planned) starting with which trade?
